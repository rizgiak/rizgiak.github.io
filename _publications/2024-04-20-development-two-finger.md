<b>Abstract:</b> We developed a two-finger robot that can grasp, rotate in-hand, and place an uneven object. Pressure sensors mounted on each finger detect the center of pressure and guide two approaches for adjusting the grasp before in-hand rotation: Object On-the-Fly Adjustment (OOA) and Re-grasp Object Movement (ROM). Evaluation experiments demonstrated the effectiveness of the proposed approaches, with ROM achieving the highest success rate of 93%.
